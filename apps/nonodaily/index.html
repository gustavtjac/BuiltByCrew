Fill cells that belong to the picture. Numbers show consecutive filled runs.

How to play: Fill in the grid so the runs of filled cells in each row/column match the clue numbers. A clue of "3 1" means there are 3 filled cells in a row, then a gap, then 1 filled cell.

Desktop: Left-click to fill, right-click to cross out (eliminated).
Mobile: Tap to fill/unfill. Use the Cross mode button, then tap to mark cells as eliminated. Long-press also marks a cross.
